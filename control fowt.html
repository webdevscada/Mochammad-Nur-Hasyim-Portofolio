Control System Design for a Floating Offshore Wind Turbine

Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) Control for a Floating Offshore Wind Turbine

Development of an active control strategy based on State-Space modeling and a Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) to improve the dynamic stability of a Floating Offshore Wind Turbine under ocean wave disturbances.

OpenFAST MATLAB Simulink State Space LQR Floating Platform

Project Summary

This project investigates the implementation of an optimal Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) controller for a Floating Offshore Wind Turbine system. The controller is designed using a state-space model to reduce platform motion while maintaining system stability under various sea-state conditions.

Project Individual Project
Software MATLAB & OpenFAST
Method State Space + LQR
Duration 2025 – 2026

Control System Design

Active Mooring Force Controller

The proposed controller is designed using a state-space model and Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR). The controller generates an optimal control force to reduce platform motion while minimizing actuator effort. The design improves the heave and pitch response of the floating platform under different wave heights.

Simulation Result

Comparison of uncontrolled and controlled Floating Offshore Wind Turbine responses under moderate and extreme wave conditions.

Wave Height 0.4 m

Heave & Pitch Displacement

The LQR controller effectively suppresses platform oscillation under moderate sea-state conditions. Both heave and pitch responses become smoother with reduced peak amplitudes.

Mooring Line Tension

After the controller is activated, the tension fluctuation in Mooring Line 2 is significantly reduced. Meanwhile,the tensions in Mooring Lines 1 and 3 slightly increase; however,they remain well below the allowable design limit of the catenary mooring system. These results indicate that the controller improves platform stability without compromising structural safety.

Wave Height 9 m

Heave & Pitch Displacement

Under severe wave excitation, the proposed controller maintains platform stability by reducing excessive motion compared with the uncontrolled system.

Mooring Line Tension

Although the platform experiences significantly larger environmental loads, the controller successfully reduces fluctuations in mooring-line tension and reduces dynamic loading on the structure.
