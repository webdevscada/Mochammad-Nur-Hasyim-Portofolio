PROJECT

Biogas

Converting Organic Waste
into Cooking Fuel

This project aims to design and develop a small-scale biogas system that utilizes organic waste to produce methane gas. The generated biogas can be used as an environmentally friendly and sustainable alternative cooking fuel.

Division & Responsibilities

IoT

Electrical System

Designed electrical wiring diagrams and assembled control panels for sensors, IoT devices, and power distribution.

Sensor Calibration

Performed calibration and validation of gas sensors prior to installation and testing on the biogas system.

Operation & Evaluation

Operated the biogas system, evaluated its performance, performed troubleshooting, and proposed improvements for future development.
