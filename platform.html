OC4 Semi-submersible

Platform Testing

This project focused on the design, dimensional determination, fabrication, assembly, and experimental testing of an OC4 Semi-submersible platform as a laboratory-scale foundation for offshore wind turbine research. The work began with developing the platform design and determining the required dimensions and specifications of its structural components. The designed platform was then fabricated and assembled before undergoing experimental testing in a pool and at sea. The tests were conducted to evaluate the platform’s buoyancy, stability, structural integrity, and overall response to environmental conditions, including waves, currents, and wind.

Project Activities

Fabrication & Testing at PPNS Pool

The project involved fabricating and assembling the main components of the OC4 Semi-submersible platform at the PPNS pool area. After fabrication, the platform was tested in the pool to evaluate its buoyancy and stability under calm conditions and artificially generated waves, while also checking for potential leakage.

Field Testing at Kenjeran Sea, Madura

The platform was tested directly in a marine environment to evaluate its performance under real operating conditions. The test focused on observing the platform's six degrees of freedom (6-DoF) motion and assessing its stability under the influence of waves, currents, and wind.

⚒

Tools & Technologies

Angle Grinder

Used for cutting and shaping metal materials and plates during the platform fabrication process.

Welding

Used to join structural components of the platform to provide sufficient strength and watertight integrity.

Autodesk Inventor

Used to develop the 3D design and structural model of the OC4 Semi-submersible platform prior to fabrication.
